# Clare Vogue current state

Updated: 2026-10-27

## Agreed product decisions

- Initial release is owner-only; require authenticator-app TOTP MFA through Supabase Auth. NestJS must require AAL2 for protected requests. Backup: a second authenticator on a separate trusted device; loss of all factors requires identity-verified provider support, never email alone.
- Each business has private customer records. Archive inactive customers; permanent personal-data deletion is a separate reviewed process that preserves or anonymizes records required for business/legal retention.
- Tailoring workflow is tracked per garment. Order progress is a derived summary. Include customer fitting, repeat alterations/fitting, dry cleaning, packaging, payment check, customer notification/collection or physical delivery. Blockers preserve stage; cancellation/rework/delivery reversal retain history. Partial delivery is tracked per garment. Delivery with a balance requires recorded customer agreement.
- Payments are recorded once and allocated to garments. Discounts are auditable price adjustments. Cancellation settlement is case-by-case; record reason, customer agreement, refund and retained amounts, bounded by amounts already paid. Clare Vogue records refunds but does not process them.
- Customer updates are copyable messages sent via the business's usual channel; customer status links are deferred. Public portfolio consent is explicit per item, dated, records method (verbal/written/electronic), and can be withdrawn; private sharing is separate.
- Measurements: ladies'-dress starter template (bust, waist, hips, shoulder width, shoulder-to-waist, sleeve length, armhole, desired dress length), custom fields, cm/in per set, hybrid named standard + validated custom fields.
- Pricing: each garment has its own price and due date; order totals/deadlines are derived. Initial business currency NGN; one currency per business. English, Africa/Lagos timezone.
- Media: attach to garment/progress update; use managed, private S3-compatible object storage with short-lived scoped direct uploads. MinIO can be used as a local compatibility service. Initial limits JPEG/PNG/WebP images 20 MB, MP4/MOV videos 200 MB; validate content.
- API: REST with OpenAPI. Dashboard due-soon window: 7 days, soonest first.
- PostgreSQL row-level security in addition to API tenant checks was selected and documented; set trusted business context transaction-locally after membership authorization, fail closed when absent, prohibit RLS bypass for the runtime database role, and test pooled-connection isolation after commit/rollback.
- Deployment direction: prefer mostly managed hosting and database services. Managed, private S3-compatible object storage is selected. Prefer a Nigeria region, with a nearby region as fallback; provider and recovery guarantees remain open.
- Recovery planning baseline: target RPO of 24 hours or better and RTO of 8 hours or better; these are not production commitments until a provider and tested procedures confirm them.
- Backup retention baseline: 30 days, subject to provider capability and cost review before launch.
- Availability planning target: at least 99.5% monthly, excluding announced maintenance and external provider outages; not a guarantee, and subject to provider capability/cost review.
- API response-time planning target: under 500 ms at the 95th percentile for ordinary dashboard, customer, and order requests under expected small-business load, excluding media transfers and external services; validate before production.
- Customer-data, media, and financial-record retention periods: intentionally deferred until local legal review and business needs are confirmed; approve related export, correction, and deletion policies before production. This is separate from system backup retention.
- Owner reminders: dashboard only in the first release; no automatic email, SMS, or push reminders. Customer-safe delivery updates are copied and sent manually through the business's usual channel.
- Launch scope: one tailoring business and one signed-in owner. Keep tenant-safe foundations, but defer multi-business onboarding and staff accounts.
- Initial volume: unknown. Plan modestly for a small business and review actual/expected garment and media usage before production sizing.
- Platform support: no routine access to business content; prefer operational metadata. Exceptional content access requires the owner's explicit approval, a time-limited session, and an audit record; define the production procedure before enabling it.
